Case Study · Mason / ModeMagic

ModeMagic — Shopify automation for D2C sellers

Redesigning Mason's flagship Shopify app from a simple badge tool into a seller-facing automation product — select badge, choose products, update store.

Role Product Designer
Scope Shopify badge automation with seller-facing workflows
Domain E-commerce · Shopify B2B
Stakeholders D2C Shopify sellers
Designers Product Designer

Problem & Context

Shopify sellers needed promo badges on hundreds of products — but had to apply them one product at a time.

The impact: During flash sales, slow and error-prone tagging meant missed windows and wrong products showing the wrong badge.

The opportunity: ModeMagic already had badge assets. The redesign had to make bulk apply and automation feel as simple as the three-step flow — select badge, choose products, update store — while giving Mason a path from experiment to a core product.

Takeaways

Early in my career, this project taught me that seller tools live or die on speed under pressure — a flash sale doesn't wait for a clunky bulk-edit flow. The three-step pattern (select → choose → apply) became the backbone for automation features that followed. I'm still proud that the experience we shipped continues to run in production.

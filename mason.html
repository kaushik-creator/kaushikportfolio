Case Study · Mason / ModeMagic

ModeMagic — Shopify automation for D2C sellers

Redesigning Mason's flagship Shopify app — ModeMagic — from a simple badge tool into a seller-facing automation product. D2C store owners use it to apply promotional badges across their catalog in three steps: select badge, choose products, update store — without touching each listing manually.

Role Product Designer
Timeline Feb 2021 – Apr 2022
Type Fulltime · E-commerce · Shopify B2B
Skills UX Design · Shopify Apps · Automation Flows · Conversion

Step 01 / Problem & Context

Badge tool → automation platform

The Problem: D2C sellers on Shopify needed to run promotions across large catalogs, but applying badges product-by-product was slow and error-prone — especially during flash sales when timing mattered.

The Opportunity: ModeMagic already had badge assets. The redesign had to make bulk application and automation feel as simple as the three-step manual flow — select badge, choose products, update store — while giving Mason a path from experiment to core product vertical.

Recognition: The redesigned experience contributed to an 8× increase in weekly active users within a year and supported a Shopify Commerce Award win for Best App User Interface — validated through product metrics and team feedback post-launch, not a controlled A/B study I ran in isolation.

Takeaways

Early in my career, this project taught me that seller tools live or die on speed under pressure — a flash sale doesn't wait for a clunky bulk-edit flow. The three-step pattern (select → choose → apply) became the backbone for automation features that followed. I'm still proud that the experience we shipped continues to run in production.

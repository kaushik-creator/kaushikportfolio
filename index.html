About me

Enterprise SaaS.
Complex systems.

UX Designer with hands-on depth in design systems, role-based access, and multi-tenant architecture. At Druva, I run design workshops, present decisions to senior leadership and engineering, and shape the interaction patterns the MSP team ships against.

How I work

  • Workshops and leadership reviews before pixels — align engineering, product, and design on the constraint, not just the mockup.
  • Map actors and access paths first — who can provision, who gets blocked, which portal owns the action.
  • Validate through partner research, usability testing, and iterative A/B testing — honest about what we measured vs. what the design enabled.

Where I'm strongest

  • Data-dense operational dashboards — backup triage, security posture, license status at a glance
  • Provisioning and access models that let partners self-serve without a support ticket ceiling
  • 0→1 design systems as founding or sole designer — Harmoney, AssetSprout, early-stage products

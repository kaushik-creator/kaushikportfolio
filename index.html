Go to →
July 2024 – Present · Fulltime
UX Designer

  • Designed the Education License model and tenant-provisioning flow for Druva MSP, introducing a 1:4 faculty-to-student licensing ratio so schools and universities pay only for faculty seats while covering their much larger student base — with license calculation, storage allocation, and billing automated end-to-end through the Managed Services Center.
